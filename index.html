KRESF — Koala Road Eye Safety Feature — explores how wildlife awareness, road design and technology could help reduce koala vehicle strikes.

Explore the mobile prototypeSee the research and design

A student-designed educational prototype — not a real-time wildlife tracker, driving aid or emergency service.

The idea

Why build KRESF?

Koalas can be vulnerable when they cross roads between habitat areas. KRESF explores how an informative map, a demonstration warning interface and better infrastructure might support wildlife protection.

The project follows an engineering design process: identify a problem, research possible solutions, build a prototype, test it, and evaluate limitations.

Safety first

This project uses illustrative demonstration locations and calculations. There is no live koala detection, validated road hazard information, or verified speed-limit guidance. Never use the app while driving. Follow road signs and official advice.

Try fencing, fauna underpasses and speed interventions in a simplified educational risk model.

🛡️

Results are illustrative model outputs, not measured probabilities or confirmed mitigation effectiveness.

Research & development

How the solution was designed

1 · InvestigateResearch koala habitats, crossing risks and wildlife rescue advice. Identify why roads can divide habitat.
2 · PrototypeDesign an educational website and mobile PWA with interactive mapping, local sighting logs and mitigation controls.
3 · Test and improveTest map behaviour, GPS permissions, installation, offline access and QR-code scanning. Record weaknesses and changes.

This digital concept is not a substitute for expert field studies, species monitoring or official road engineering design. Research references and testing evidence should be provided alongside the school presentation.
